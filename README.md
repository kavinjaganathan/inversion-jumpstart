Inversion

Inversion is a 2D platformer I made in Godot where you can flip gravity at any time. Walk on the ceiling, dodge spikes, grab coins, and try not to fall into the void. There are three levels, and each one leans harder on the gravity flip than the last.

Play it in your browser: https://itzoofywastaken.itch.io/inversion

Controls
A / D to move
Space to jump (hold it to jump higher)
Shift to flip gravity
Shift + R to restart

How it works
You need every coin in a level before the exit opens. Spikes and the void each cost you a life, and you get three. There's a timer running the whole time, so you can try to beat your own best run.

Running it locally
Download Godot 4, open project.godot, and hit F5.

Everything in the game (platforms, coins, spikes, UI) is built in code, so there are no art assets in the repo.
